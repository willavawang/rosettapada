Sandalwood, tagara, blue lotus, jasmine:of all these scents,the fragrance of ethical conductis unsurpassed.

Pāli, verse 55

Candanaṁ tagaraṁ vāpi,uppalaṁ atha vassikī;Etesaṁ gandhajātānaṁ,sīlagandho anuttaro.

Patna, verse 55, Patna 122 [8.2] Puṣpa

candanaṁ tagaraṁ cāpiuppalaṁ atha vāśśikiṁ |etesāṁ gandhajātānāṁśīlagandho anuttaro ||

Gāndhārī, verse 55, Gāndhārī 296 [18.7] [Puṣpa], partial correspondence

. . . . . . ya vi. . . . . . . .. . . ganajadaṇaśilagano ivutama.

Sanskrit, verse 55, Udānavarga 6.17 Śīla, partial correspondence

tagarāc candanāc cāpivārṣikāyās tathotpalāt |etebhyo gandhajātebhyaḥśīlagandhas tv anuttaraḥ ||

Chinese, verse 55, T210 12.13

旃檀多香，青蓮芳花，雖曰是真，不如戒香。

Chinese pinyin, verse 55, T210 12.13

zhān tán duō xiāng，qīng lián fāng huā，suī yuē shì zhēn，bù rú jiè xiāng。

Verse 56

Faint is the scentof tagara and sandalwood.The fragrance of those who live wellreaches even the gods.

Appamatto ayaṁ gandho,yāyaṁ tagaracandanī;Yo ca sīlavataṁ gandho,vāti devesu uttamo.

Verse 57

English, verse 57

Māra cannot find the pathof those who live with heedfulness,whose conduct is completeand who are free through right understanding.

Pāli, verse 57

Tesaṁ sampannasīlānaṁ,appamādavihārinaṁ;Sammadaññāvimuttānaṁ,māro maggaṁ na vindati.

Patna, verse 57, Patna 124 [8.4] Puṣpa

Gāndhārī, verse 57, Gāndhārī 297 [18.8] [Puṣpa], partial correspondence

. . . baṇaśilaṇaapramadavihariṇasamadañavimutaṇagadi maro na vinadi.

Sanskrit, verse 57, Udānavarga 6.19 Śīla

teṣāṁ viśuddhaśīlānāmapramādavihāriṇām /samyagājñāvimuktānāṁmāro mārgaṁ na vindati ||

Chinese, verse 57, T210 12.15

戒具成就，行無放逸，定意度脫，長離魔道。

Chinese pinyin, verse 57, T210 12.15

jiè jù chéng jiù，xíng wú fàng yì，dìng yì dù tuō，cháng lí mó dào。

Tibetan, verse 57, Toh 326 · Derge 72, 216a.2–216a.3

Verse 58

English, verse 58

From refuse thrown downbeside a busy roada lotus may rise,pure in fragrance, a delight to the heart.
