Verse 1

English, verse 1

Phenomena are preceded by mind.Mind is foremost; they are fashioned by mind.If someone, with a corrupted mind,speaks or acts,then suffering follows them,as the wheel follows the draft-ox’s foot.

Pāli, verse 1

manopubbaṅgamā dhammā,manoseṭṭhā manomayā,manasā ce paduṭṭhenabhāsati vā karoti vā,tato naṁ dukkham anveticakkaṁ va vahato padaṁ.

Patna, verse 1

manopūrvvaṁgamā dhammāmanośreṣṭhā manojavāmanasā ca praduṣṭenabhāṣate vā karoti vātato naṁ dukham anneticakram vā vahato padaṁ

Gāndhārī, verse 1

maṇopuvagama dhamamaṇośeṭha maṇojavamaṇasa hi praduṭheṇabhaṣadi va karodi vatado ṇa duhu amedicako va vahaṇe pathi.

Sanskrit, verse 1

manaḥpūrvaṅgamā dharmāmanaḥśreṣṭhā manojavāḥmanasā hi praduṣṭenabhāṣate vā karoti vātatas taṁ duḥkham anveticakraṁ vā vahataḥ padam

Chinese, verse 1

心為法本，心尊心使，中心念惡，即言即行，罪苦自追，車轢于轍。

Chinese pinyin, verse 1

xīn wéi fǎ běn,xīn zūn xīn shǐ,zhōng xīn niàn è,jí yán jí xíng,zuì kǔ zì zhuī,chē lì yú zhé.

Verse 2

English, verse 2

Phenomena are preceded by mind.Mind is foremost; they are fashioned by mind.If someone, with a clear mind,speaks or acts,then happiness follows them,like a shadow that never leaves.

Pāli, verse 2

manopubbaṅgamā dhammā,manoseṭṭhā manomayā,manasā ce pasannenabhāsati vā karoti vā,tato naṁ sukham anvetichāyā va anapāyinī.

Patna, verse 2

manopūrvvaṁgamā dhammāmanośreṣṭhā manojavāmanasā ca prasannenabhāṣate vā karoti vātato naṁ sukham anneticchāyā vā anapāyinī

Gāndhārī, verse 2

maṇopuvagama dhamamaṇośeṭha maṇojavamaṇasa hi prasaneṇabhaṣadi va karodi vatado ṇa suhu amedichaya va aṇukamiṇi.

Sanskrit, verse 2

manaḥpūrvaṅgamā dharmāmanaḥśreṣṭhā manojavāḥmanasā hi prasannenabhāṣate vā karoti vātatas taṁ sukham anveticchāyā vā hy anugāminī

Chinese, verse 2

心為法本，心尊心使，中心念善，即言即行，福樂自追，如影隨形。

Chinese pinyin, verse 2

xīn wéi fǎ běn,xīn zūn xīn shǐ,zhōng xīn niàn shàn,jí yán jí xíng,fú lè zì zhuī,rú yǐng suí xíng.

How to read

The Dhammapada in Pāli, set verse by verse beside its surviving parallels in Patna Prakrit, Gāndhārī, Sanskrit, Chinese and Tibetan, with English alongside.

  • MoveScroll down for the next verse, across for the other traditions.

  • +−F

    ZoomPinch, or press + and −. 0 returns to reading size; F fits every column.

  • OriginalEnglish

    EnglishThe switch at the top shows every historical text in its own language or in English (E also flips it). The English column stays put.

  • Chinese

    ColumnsDrag a heading left or right. × hides a column; + at the end of the row brings it back.

  • []

    VersesStep verse by verse. Links to the next chapter follow the last verse.
