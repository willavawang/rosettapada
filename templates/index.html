Pan
Two-finger scroll in either direction, or hold Space and drag.
Zoom
Pinch on a trackpad, or use + / −. 0 returns to reading size.
Turn
The arrows in the bottom bar, or [ / ], move between verses and keep your place across the sheet.

Open Languages to show or hide columns. Drag a language row to reorder it; click its checkbox to show or hide it.
