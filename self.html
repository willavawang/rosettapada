Verse 157

English, verse 157

If you know that you are dear to yourself,keep good watch over yourself.Through at least one of the night’s three watches,a wise person would stay alert.

Pāli, verse 157

Attānañce piyaṁ jaññā,rakkheyya naṁ surakkhitaṁ;Tiṇṇaṁ aññataraṁ yāmaṁ,paṭijaggeyya paṇḍito.

Verse 158

English, verse 158

First establish yourselfin what is fitting.Then teach others.In this way the wise avoid corruption.

Chinese pinyin, verse 158, T210 20.2

As a creeper smothers a sal tree,utter misconduct overtakes a person.They do to themselveswhat an enemy wishes upon them.

Verse 163

English, verse 163

It is easy to do what is wrongand harmful to yourself.What is good and beneficialis exceedingly hard to do.

Chinese pinyin, verse 163, T210 20.7

Verse 164

English, verse 164

A fool who clings to a harmful viewand rejects the teaching of the arahants,the noble ones who live by the Dhamma,bears fruit only to their own undoing,like a bamboo that fruits and dies.

Pāli, verse 164

Yo sāsanaṁ arahataṁ,ariyānaṁ dhammajīvinaṁ;Paṭikkosati dummedho,diṭṭhiṁ nissāya pāpikaṁ;Phalāni kaṭṭhakasseva,attaghātāya phallati.

Sanskrit, verse 164, Udānavarga 8.7 Vāca

Chinese, verse 164, T210 20.8, partial correspondence

如真人教，以道法身，愚者疾之，見而為惡。行惡得惡，如種苦種，

Chinese pinyin, verse 164, T210 20.8, partial correspondence

Tibetan, verse 164, Toh 326 · Derge 72, 217a.1–217a.2

བསམ་བརླག་གང་ཞིག་སྡིག་ཅན་གྱི། །ལྟ་ལ་བརྟེན་ནས་འཕགས་པ་ནི། །དགྲ་བཅོམ་ཆོས་ཀྱིས་འཚོ་བ་དང་། །བསྟན་པ་སྤོང་བར་བྱེད་པ་དག །སྨྱིག་མ་འབྲུ་ཆགས་ལྟ་བུར་ནི། །དེ་ནི་རང་གི་འབྲས་བུས་གསོད། །
