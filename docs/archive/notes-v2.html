DHAMMAPADA ROSETTA / DESIGN STUDY 02

Reading notes

The seven reading columns appear together on one continuous, unruled sheet. There are no table borders, panels, or sticky English column. The underlying layout still uses aligned line groups: these preserve comparisons when text wraps, without making the work look like a spreadsheet.

How to move

Two-finger trackpad scrolling moves across the sheet horizontally and vertically, provided the browser sends both scroll axes. Use pinch to zoom in browsers that support trackpad pinch as a gesture, or use the visible + and − controls. Hold Space while dragging with a mouse or trackpad press to pan. Arrow buttons at the bottom move between verses, not languages; the Contents link opens a separate index page. Press 0 to reset zoom, + / − to adjust.

Why some cells are empty

"Not yet verified" means that this particular source reading has not yet been transcribed and verified for the prototype. It must never be conflated with an actual absence of a verse from that tradition.

A sample of meaningful variation

The Pāli has manomayā (“made by mind”), while Patna, Gāndhārī, and Sanskrit have forms of manojavā (“impelled by mind”). The English column is a provisional, new working rendering of Pāli, not a neutral translation of every variant.

The collections do not share the same original ordering. These verses correspond to Pāli Dhammapada 1–2, Patna 1–2, Gāndhārī 201–202, Sanskrit Udānavarga 31.23–24, and Chinese Faju jing T210 9.1–2. Tibetan text remains to be verified from a reliable source.
