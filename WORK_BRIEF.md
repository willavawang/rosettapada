# Rosettapada — Work brief

## Current phase: reading experience, study 06

Renamed from Dhammapada Rosetta on 23 October 2026. The user has now requested three complete pages in the usual Pāli order: Pairs, Heedfulness, and The Mind. This authorizes the bounded addition of Pāli verses 1–43 and an existing English translation. It does not authorize full-corpus research or the six-tradition concordance.

`index.html` preserves the original comparative sample. `pairs.html` (1–20), `heedfulness.html` (21–32), and `mind.html` (33–43) use Bhikkhu Sujato's English and the Mahāsaṅgīti Pāli from a pinned, CC0-licensed SuttaCentral/Bilara snapshot. `data/chapters.json` records the exact revision, source paths, Git blob IDs, and checksums. Source text remains offline and reproducible. Keep English wording distinct from the original sample's working translation.

## Reading requirements

1. One continuous, borderless sheet. English, Pāli, Patna, Gāndhārī, Sanskrit, Chinese, and Tibetan remain horizontally aligned. No visible table grid, cards, or fixed English pane.
2. Legibility takes priority over fitting. Default English is 22px; historical romanized text is 21px. Horizontal overflow is intentional. Fit is an overview, not a reading-size default.
3. Use native two-axis trackpad scrolling. Support pointer-centered pinch/gesture magnification and accessible zoom buttons. Preserve ordinary text selection; Space+drag is the optional pointer pan.
4. Previous/next verse arrows sit in the fixed bottom bar. They retain horizontal reading position and use addressable verse anchors. Remove visible verse labels and editorial titles; leave a compact stanza gap.
5. Contents links directly to the three chapter pages with an up-right arrow; do not list verses. Verse arrows continue across chapter boundaries, retaining zoom and horizontal reading position. Do not imply the rest of the catalogue is available.
6. Keep the reading view quiet. Remove contextual taglines and introductory eyebrows. The wordmark is Rosettapada with no period. Put source explanations and uncertainty in Notes; Tibetan remains explicitly “not yet verified.”
7. The bottom bar uses icons: a globe opens a window-style language panel, and a diagonal expand symbol fits visible columns. Keep useful magnification feedback. Hide scrollbars everywhere without disabling native scrolling.
8. Language checkboxes show/hide columns without empty gaps. The entire language row except its checkbox supports dragging to reorder both headings and poems; quiet handles also support keyboard reordering. Persist choices. Modern Mandarin pinyin for the two Chinese sample verses is an optional reading aid, hidden by default; it is not another historical tradition.

## Review before broader milestones

Evaluate line spacing, column widths, panning at 100%, Fit versus reading size, verse changes from the right side of the sheet, and the contents round trip. Physically test Safari trackpad gestures on the user's Mac before treating that path as approved.

The comparative sample's English remains a provisional Pāli-based rendering. The new chapter pages attribute their English to Bhikkhu Sujato. Horizontal alignment is a reading aid, not a word-by-word or cross-tradition concordance. Preserve the inherited sample and references. Keep edition-specific line breaks, omitted commentary-derived story headings, title choices, and textual uncertainties documented in Notes.

Keep this project separate from the Buddhist Scripture Atlas. Its source repository is `https://github.com/willavawang/rosettapada`. The original brief is retained in `docs/archive/WORK_BRIEF-v2.md`.
