About the reading sample

Reading notes

One teaching, encountered through different texts.

What you are reading

The first two Pāli Dhammapada verses, with provisional parallel readings from Patna, Gāndhārī, Sanskrit, and Chinese. Tibetan has a place on the sheet, but its source and reading are not yet verified. This status does not imply that a parallel is absent.

The English is a working rendering of Pāli. It does not translate all six traditions. Verse titles are editorial reading aids.

Alignment is a reading aid

Lines sit beside one another to help the eye compare. This is a provisional correspondence by sense, not a verified line-by-line concordance. Spelling, punctuation, source numbering, and the transcriptions still require editorial verification.

The sample labels its witnesses as Pāli Dhp 1–2; Patna PDhp 1–2; Gāndhārī GDhp 201–202; Sanskrit Udānavarga 31.23–24; and Chinese Faju jing, T210 9.1–2. These are inherited references, retained for review.

A difference worth keeping visible

The sample has Pāli manomayā (“made by mind”), alongside forms of manojavā in Patna, Gāndhārī, and Sanskrit, provisionally glossed “impelled by mind.” The English line follows the Pāli reading. The wording and this gloss await source review; visual alignment should not erase such differences.
