What you are reading

Contents opens the first three chapters in the usual Pāli order: Pairs (verses 1–20), Heedfulness (21–32), and The Mind (33–43). These pages place Bhikkhu Sujato’s English translation beside the Mahāsaṅgīti Pāli edition, as published by SuttaCentral. The translation is dedicated to the public domain under CC0.

The earlier comparative sample remains available with its original two verses and working English rendering. Its Patna, Gāndhārī, Sanskrit, and Chinese readings are provisional. Tibetan remains not yet verified; this does not imply that a parallel is absent. The new chapter pages do not extend the historical concordance.

Chapter titles and line breaks

Yamakavagga is the chapter of pairs or twin verses; Appamādavagga concerns heedfulness, also rendered diligence or earnestness; Cittavagga concerns the mind, called thought in some older translations. These English titles translate the traditional Pāli chapter names. Our second page uses “Heedfulness”; Sujato’s source heading is “Diligence.”

Verse wording and line divisions on the three new pages follow the selected SuttaCentral edition. Surrounding whitespace and nonprinting formatting markers are omitted, as are the source’s commentary-derived story headings and chapter-ending colophons. Line breaks belong to this edition’s presentation, not an independently verified reconstruction of an original manuscript.

Matching source segments are placed side by side for reading. English word order can differ from Pāli: in verses 42–43, for example, corresponding words do not occupy matching visual lines. This is not a word-by-word concordance.

The comparative sample

Lines in the earlier multilingual sample form a provisional correspondence by sense, not a verified line-by-line concordance. Its spelling, punctuation, source numbering, and transcriptions still require editorial verification. Its English is a working rendering of Pāli and does not translate all six traditions.

The sample labels its witnesses as Pāli Dhp 1–2; Patna PDhp 1–2; Gāndhārī GDhp 201–202; Sanskrit Udānavarga 31.23–24; and Chinese Faju jing, T210 9.1–2. These are inherited references, retained for review.

A difference worth keeping visible

The sample has Pāli manomayā (“made by mind”), alongside forms of manojavā in Patna, Gāndhārī, and Sanskrit, provisionally glossed “impelled by mind.” The English line follows the Pāli reading. The wording and this gloss await source review; visual alignment should not erase such differences.
